Sales DB holds the customer master, every order and every refund of a European B2B business: 40 customers across FR, DE, ES, IT and the UK, 411 orders from 2 January to 1 July 2026, and 43 refunds. Use it for revenue, order volume, refunds, channel and regional performance, and customer segments (SMB, Enterprise). Amounts are in euros, VAT included. It has no product lines, costs or margins, and no marketing data — campaigns and account managers live in the CRM source, joined on the customer id.
